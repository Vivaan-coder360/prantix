ABOUT PRANTIX

The story behind Prantix

Prantix started with a simple idea: building software should feel exciting, not intimidating.

01

A young developer with a big idea

This website was created by a young developer named Vivaan Panwar at the age of 10. Using a Dell Latitude 7480, he set out to build his own website and turn his ideas into something real.

The journey hasn't always been easy. There have been challenges along the way, including problems such as the laptop crashing and having to figure out how to keep moving forward.

02

Why Prantix?

Prantix began as a personal project and a way to learn, experiment, and create something of his own.

The goal is to keep improving the project, learning from mistakes, and seeing how far an idea can go when you keep building.

Start small. Build something real.

Every project starts somewhere. Prantix is one developer's attempt to take an idea and turn it into something bigger, one step at a time.
